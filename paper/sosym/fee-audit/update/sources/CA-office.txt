Patent fees
Different fees must be paid during the patent application process, and after a patent is granted. The amount of those fees is shown in the lists below.
Fee adjustments – what fee do I pay?
For fees subject to an adjustment, the fee amount you must pay depends on the date which the fee is received by the Commissioner Footnote 1 , not the date which the service was originally requested, nor the prescribed date to pay the fee.
How to determine what fee to pay
For all fees subject to an adjustment:
If payment is received before January 1 of next year, you will need to pay the prescribed fee for the current year.
If payment is received on or after January 1 of next year, you will need to pay the adjusted prescribed fee for next year.
For example, if, after you have filed a Canadian application and obtained a filing date, but have not paid the application fee and a notice was sent prior to January of the next year, the amount you should pay will depend on when you pay it. If, in response to the notice, you pay the full amount prior to January, then you must pay the prescribed amount for the current year. If you have paid on or after January 1 of the next year, then you must pay the adjusted prescribed fee for next year.
Similarly, the amount due for a maintenance fee depends on the date which your payment is received, not the prescribed date to pay the fee. This applies whether the fee is paid early, during the late fee period, or during the reinstatement period.

Annual fee adjustment – 2026
On January 1, 2026 , certain fees related to patent applications and patents increased by 2.7%, in accordance with the Service Fees Act
As a result, the amounts shown in the Tariff of Fees of the Patent Rules , made available to the public by the Department of Justice Canada, may not be up to date. However, the fee amounts shown on this page are up to date.
Please note that not all fees are subject to an annual adjustment. To view fee amounts that were in effect prior to 2026, consult our list of historical patent fees .
Small entity vs. standard fees
Patent fees are shown in 2 categories:
Small entity fees – Available to applicants and patentees that meet the small entity condition and who submit a valid small entity declaration within the prescribed time. Eligible applicants include entities with fewer than 100 employees and universities.
Standard fees – Applies in all other cases
To learn more, consult section 10.02 of the Manual of Patent Office Practice.
Note: Certain fees are not reduced for small entities and therefore their fee amounts appear the same in both the small entity and standard fee columns
