Taxes applicable to patents and certificates of addition
TITLE I: APPLICABLE TAXES ON PATENTS AND CERTIFICATES OF ADDITION
Title: Taxes applicable to patents and certificates of addition | New taxes in CFA francs (1 euro = 655.957)
1- Fees for obtaining patents and Certificates of Addition
a) Filing (paper) of patent covering the first year | 225 000
b) Filing (paper) of certificate of addition | 285 000
c) Claim of priority, by priority | 75 000
d) Publication of a patent application or certificate of addition | 365 000
e) Publication of the patent or certificate of addition | 150 000
f) Objection or claim of ownership | 250 000
g) Examination of the application | 250 000
h) Issuance of the patent or certificate of addition | 200 000
i) Supplement per claim beyond the 10th | 65 000
j) Length of the descriptive report
• 11 to 20 typed pages or drawings (A4 format) | 120 000
• 21 to 30 pages or plates | 300 000
From 31 to 40 pages or plates. Beyond that, at a rate of 80,000 FCFA per undivided increment of 10 pages or plates | 600 000
k) Filing of the divisional application NB: In terms of fees, divisional applications are subject to the same fees as initial applications | 225 000
l) Correction of hardware errors:
• Correction of an error on the form before publication (Registerer's error) | 40 000
• Correction of hardware errors after publication | 60 000
(Depositor's Error)
m) Editing a page
• Modification of a page of the description, drawing plates or summary | 40 000
• Modifying a claims page following the deletion of one or more claims | 40 000
n) Modification or addition of a claim | 65 000
o) Transformation of an application for a certificate of addition into a patent application | 105 000
p) Transformation of a utility model registration application into a patent application | 560 000
2- Fees for maintaining patents in force
Patent annuities
• From year 2 to year 5, by year | 270 000
• From 6th to 10th grade per year | 400 000
• From 11th to 15th grade, by year | 525 000
• From grade 16 to grade 20, by year | 675 000
• Surcharge for late payment of annuity(ies) | 70 000
Patent validation fees NB: validation fees will be determined by mutual agreement with the partners | PM
Patent publication fee | PM
3- Rights Restoration Taxes
• For fault attributable to the agent | 650 000
• For other circumstances beyond the control of the holder | 375 000
• Fee for publishing restoration decisions, per decision | 60 000
4- Fees relating to the special patent register
a) Registration in the special patent register | 265 000
b) Issuance of a copy of the decision regarding any entry in the Special Register of Patents | 60 000
5-Research areas
a) Research relating to the filing of a patent application or certificate of addition | 110 000
b) Search relating to a patent or certificate of addition | 110 000
c) Literature review on the state of the art | 200 000
d) Search on a patent holder's patent portfolio | 120 000
6- Fees for obtaining information
a) Issuance of an official copy of the description and drawings or priority documents of a patent application or a certificate of addition
• Up to 10 pages or plates | 60 000
• Above 10 pages or plates (per additional page or plate) | 15 000
b) Certified copy of a decision granting a patent or certificate of addition | 80 000
c) Certified copy of a decision of Registration in the Special Register of Patents or certificate of addition | 80 000
d) Certified copy of a decision of opposition, restoration, claim of ownership, rejection, appeal, arbitration, etc. | 80 000
e) The registration status in the Special Register of Patents or Certificates of Addition | 110 000
(f) Duplicate of a decision granting a patent or a certificate of addition | 80 000
(g) Certified copy of a patent booklet or certificate of addition: From 1 to 10 pages; Beyond 10 pages, in indivisible increments of 10 pages | 55 000 10 000
h) Issuance of a statement of annuity payments | 110 000
(i) Other information on a patent or certificate of addition | 110 000
(j) Fee for providing a copy of an original of a patent application or a certificate of addition, fixed as follows: From 1 to 10 pages; Beyond 10 pages, per indivisible increment of 10 pages | 30 000 2 500
7 – Appeal fee, per appeal
Appeal fee, per appeal | 960 000
8- Extension taxes
• From OAPI to a new Member State – Patents | 125 000
– Certificates of addition | 150 000
• From a new member state to OAPI
– Patents | 250 000
– Certificates of addition | 300 000
• Supplement for late application for extension from a new State to OAPI or from OAPI to a new State | 70 000
• Correction of hardware errors on the extension form | 25 000
9- Other taxes
Change of representative, by patent application. Fee for processing and transmitting an international PCT application (OAPI receiving office) to the International Bureau (WIPO/Geneva) | 90 000 50 000
