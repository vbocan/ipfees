Changes in the amount of Eurasian fees
Updated: 14.01.2025 13:45 (GMT+3)
Certain fees in the Eurasian Patent Organization will be amended as of February 1, 2025.
Due to inflation and exchange rate volatility, certain fees for Eurasian applications and patents for inventions have been indexed, including fees for filing applications, conducting examinations, amending and modifying documents, filing appeals, extending missed time limits, restoring rights, etc.
The unitary procedural fee will amount to 60,000 rubles. Find out more about the new fees in the “Statute on Fees.”
Applicants from the EAPO Member States are granted reduced tariffs: state universities and educational organizations shall pay 30% of the total amount of fees; natural persons shall pay 10% of the total amount of fees. Legal entities receive a 10% discount when paying fees.
