Forms and fees for new applications
Filing a patent application in Slovakia
You can apply for a patent for an invention by filing an application with the IPO SK. The application must be made in writing - it is recommended to use the form - Application ( Žiadosť ) or to use the electronic filing services of the Office*.
The Office's electronic services ( Elektronické služby úradu ) are available through three systems:
The Electronic Services Portal (PES of the SR Office)
The Electronic Services Portal
Central Portal of Public Administration
* electronic submissions are eligible for a fee discount pursuant to Section 6 of Act No. 145/1995 Coll. on administrative fees, as amended
FORMS
Necessary documents, such as the Application for the Grant of a Patent for an Invention or the Application for the Registration of a Change of Name/Name/Location/Address of the Patent Owner/Applicant/Patent Originator are available on the Patents/Documents Downloads subpage ( Patenty / Dokumenty na stiahnutie ) .
