The procedure in the national Phase
OA.01 TRANSLATION (CORRECTION)
Errors in the translation of the international application can be corrected with reference to the text of the international application as filed (refer to National Phase, paragraphs NP 6.002 and NP 6.003 ). Any correction of an error on the application after entry into the OAPI regional phase entails the payment of a related correction fee.
OA.02 FEES (MANNER OF PAYMENT)
The complete lists of fees charged by the Office for patents and utility models are available at the following addresses:
https://oapi.int/en/the-applicable-taxes-on-patents-and-certificates-of-addition/
https://oapi.int/en/applicable-taxes-on-utility-models/ The payment of fees to OAPI must be made in CFA francs. Fees can be paid in cash, by bank check, bank transfer, deposit into an account, or electronic payment. All payments must include the application number (the regional number assigned by the Office if already known, or the international number if the regional number is not yet known).
OA.03 ANNUAL FEES
BAR I Art. 44 al. 1 , BAR I Art. 44 al. 2 , AI No 1.48 , AI No 1.49 , AI No 1.50 , AI No 1.51 Annual fees must be paid for each year, starting from the first anniversary of the international filing date. For the payment deadlines regarding annual fees for the second and third years, please refer to the Summary. Payment of annual fees for subsequent years must be made no later than each anniversary date of the international filing. Payment may still be made within a period of six months after the deadline, subject to a surcharge for late payment. For the amounts, refer to the links in paragraph OA.02 and to Annex OA.I.
OA.04 POWER OF ATTORNEY
BAR Art. 8 al. 4 , AI No 0.17 , AI No 0.18 , AI No 0.19 , AI No 0.20 , AI No 0.21 An agent must be appointed by filing a power of attorney. A model is given in Annex OA.II.
OA.05 EXAMINATION
BAR art. 10, BAR I art. 23, BAR II art. 21 The Office examines patent applications and applications for utility model registration certificates as to substance. The examination is subject to the payment of a fee, the amount of which is specified in the Office's schedule of fees (refer to the links in paragraph OA.02 and to Annex OA.I).
OA.06 AMENDMENT OF THE APPLICATION; TIME LIMITS
PCT Article 28 , PCT Article 41 The applicant may amend the description, claims and drawings of the application, provided that the subject matter of the application is not broadened thereby, until the later of the following two events: (i) the expiration of six months from the time limit applicable under PCT Article 22 or 39(1), (ii) the final decision of OAPI to grant a patent. The amendments must be made by means of replacement sheets and are subject to the payment of a fee whose amount is indicated in the Office's schedule of fees (refer to the links in paragraph OA.02 and to Annex OA.I).
OA.07 REVIEW UNDER ARTICLE 25 OF THE PCT
PCT Article 25 , PCT Rule 51 , BAR I art. 27 al. 8 , RHCA art. 1 , RHCA art. 2 The applicable procedure is outlined in paragraphs NP 6.018 to NP 6.021 of the National Phase. If, upon review under PCT Article 25, OAPI denies an error or an omission on the part of the receiving Office or the International Bureau, an appeal against this decision may be lodged within one month from the notification of the decision. A fee for appeal (refer to Annex OA.I) must be paid at the same time. The High Commission of Appeal of OAPI will then decide on the appeal.
OA.08 EXCUSE OF DELAYS IN MEETING TIME LIMITS
PCT Article 24(2) , PCT Article 48(2) , BAR I art. 45 , RR art. 5 Reference is made to paragraphs NP 6.022 to NP 6.027 of the National Phase. Where the applicant, in spite of all due care required by the circumstances having been taken, was unable to meet the time limits for entry into the national phase under PCT Article 22 or 39, he may file a request for restoration of his rights. Likewise, if he was not able to meet the time limit for payment of an annual fee for causes beyond his control, he may file a request for restoration of his rights at the latest 24 months after the expiration of the time limit.
OA.09 UTILITY MODEL
PCT Article 4(3) , PCT Article 43 , BAR II Art. 22(3) Subject to what is said in paragraph OA.11, if the applicant wishes to obtain a utility model registration certificate instead of a patent invention, on the basis of an international application, it must be indicated in the international application (in Box No. V of the request) when filed. However, the OAPI does not issue both a patent and a utility model registration certificate for the same invention.
OA.10
BAR II Art. 22(3) The requirements for the national phase are basically the same as for patents, except that it is the fees for utility models which must be paid.
OA.11
BAR II art. 12 , BAR II art. 20 , AI 2.21 Where, in the case referred to in paragraph OA.08, the international application (i) is not restricted to a single principal subject, the applicant must, at the latest before the expiration of six months from the date of the notification from OAPI that the application cannot be accepted as presented on the grounds of not having one principal subject, divide the application into the required number of applications benefiting from the international filing date; PCT Rule 7 .2 , PCT Article 7(2)(ii) (ii) does not contain drawings, the applicant must furnish the drawings within two months from the date of the notification from OAPI. Where an international application for a patent is converted into a utility model application (refer to the next paragraph), the drawing(s) must be submitted with the request for conversion.
OA.12 CONVERSION
BAR I art. 12 , BAR II art. 20 , AI 2.21 An international application for a patent may be converted into a utility model application after the applicant has compiled with the requirements for entering the national phase for a patent application as indicated in the Summary. The conversion is subject to the payment of a fee for conversion indicated in the Office's schedule of fees (refer to the links in paragraph OA.02 and to Annex OA.I).

OA.05 EXAMINATION
BAR art. 10, BAR I art. 23, BAR II art. 21
The Office examines patent applications and applications for utility model registration certificates as to substance. The examination is subject to the payment of a fee, the amount of which is specified in the Office's schedule of fees (refer to the links in paragraph OA.02 and to Annex OA.I).
OA.06 AMENDMENT OF THE APPLICATION; TIME LIMITS
The amendments must be made by means of replacement sheets and are subject to the payment of a fee whose amount is indicated in the Office's schedule of fees (refer to the links in paragraph OA.02 and to Annex OA.I).

OA.09 UTILITY MODEL
BAR II Art. 22(3)
Subject to what is said in paragraph OA.11, if the applicant wishes to obtain a utility model registration certificate instead of a patent invention, on the basis of an international application, it must be indicated in the international application (in Box No. V of the request) when filed.
However, the OAPI does not issue both a patent and a utility model registration certificate for the same invention.
