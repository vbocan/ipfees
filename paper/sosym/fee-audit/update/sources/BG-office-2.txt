         (4) The fees for filing, examination and appeals against decisions on applications for inventions, as well as the fee for
filing a utility model application, shall be paid at 50 per cent of the prescribed amount where the applicants are inventors, micro
and small enterprises within the meaning of the Small and Medium-sized Enterprises Act, state or municipal schools, state higher
education institutions, or publicly funded academic research organizations.
