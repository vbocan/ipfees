2. International search
I. When Nordic Patent Institute (NPI) is Searching Authority:
a. Basic fee
DKK 14,080
b. If an earlier search from the Danish, Norwegian, Finnish or Icelandic priority application is requested used in the international application, you can get a refund at 25%
c. If an earlier international search or an international type search carried out by NPI, PRV or EPO is requested used in the international application, you can get a refund at 50%
Il. When the Swedish Patent Office (PRV) is Searching Authority:
a. Basic fee
DKK 14,080
b. If an earlier search from the Danish, Norwegian, Finnish or Icelandic priority application is requested used in the international application, you can get a refund at 2800 SEK
c. If an earlier international search or an international type search carried out by NPI, PRV or EPO is requested used in the international application, you can get a respectively refund at 0%, 50% or 100%
IIl. When the European Patent Office (EPO) is Searching Authority:
a. Basic fee
DKK 14,080
b. If an earlier international search or an international type search carried out by NPI, PRV or EPO is requested used in the international application, you can get a respectively refund at 25%, 50%, 75% or 100%
