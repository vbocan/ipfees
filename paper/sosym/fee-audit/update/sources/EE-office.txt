Portal of electronic filing of applications for legal protection of industrial property
New applications for registration of a patent, a utility model, applications for validation of the European patent can be filled in the Portal and sent to the Estonian Patent Office.
E-Services (trade marks and industrial designs)
E-applications concerning legal protection of trade marks and industrial designs, renewal, changes in the data and transfer of rights and ownership can be filed with the Estonian Patent Office via the portal of e-services.
Moreover it is possible to file electronic revocation applications of trade marks to the Industrial Property Board of Appeal in this portal.
Madrid eFiling
Through the portal, you can submit an international e-application under the Madrid Protocol, provided that you already have an Estonian national application or registration for the same trade mark.

Payment of the fee by a person not residing or operating in Estonia
The Estonian Patent Act and Utility Models Act permit the payment of state fees if the following legal requirements are fulfilled:
The official language of the Estonian Patent Office is Estonian. So all the documents shall be filed in Estonian.
The Estonian Patent Office communicates only with the representative, who has been registered in the Estonian state register of patent attorneys, even in case a problem arises. For example if the state fee is paid in an amount less than the prescribed rate and there is no patent attorney the patent becomes invalid.
The state fee is deemed to be paid upon receipt by the Patent Office of following information certifying payment of the state fee :
the number and date of the payment document;
the name of the payer;
the amount paid;
action for which the state fee is being paid for;
number of the patent application or registration;
confirmation of the bank or signature of the person who composed the document;
it should be evident on the document which institution or person composed it.
The document certifying payment of the state fee shall be submitted to e-mail address [email protected] or sent by post to Tatari 39, Tallinn 15041, Estonia.
The Estonian Patent Office does not send any notifications if the state fee is not paid by the due date. If the document certifying payment of the state fee has not been submitted on time the patent becomes invalid.
According to the Estonian Trade Mark Act a person with no residence, seat or commercial or industrial enterprise operating in Estonia shall authorize a patent attorney who has been registered in the Estonian state register of patent attorneys as the person’s representative to perform procedures related to payment of renewal fees.
