Fees of the Danish Patent and Trademark Office per 1 January 2026

Prices are listed in Danish kroner.

If you are uncertain about the fee, you will be able to find and pay the correct amount
in the Danish Patent and Trademark Office's self-service payment solution PVS
Pay using one of the following accepted credit cards: Dankort, VISA/Dankort,
EURO/Mastercard, American Express, VISA, VISA Electron, JCB and MobilePay.
