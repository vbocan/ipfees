Pay Annual Fee
Through this service, applicants/owners are able to pay annual fees, to maintain their industrial property rights, regardless of whether the application is under consideration or has been actioned.
Service Fees
Annual maintenance fee within 3 months from the due date
No fees required (but the payment process must be completed)
Penalties
Late charges (within 3 months from the due date)
AED 200/month for individuals
AED 400/month for companies
Retrieval of application (after 6 months from the due date)
No fees required (but the payment process must be completed)
Conditions and Requirements
The annual fee is calculated from the date of submitting the application.
For the national phase of Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) application, it is calculated retroactively from the international filing date.
Late charges are imposed after the three month grace period; and are charged monthly for 3 months (up to AED 600 for individuals and AED 1,200 for companies).
Required Documents
N/A

Fill in the application data
